The Hidden Problem

Why Your Airways Feel Congested & Restricted
— And How LungFresh Helps

Every breath you take draws in not just oxygen, but microscopic particles — pollution, allergens, dust, and airborne irritants that accumulate in airway tissue over time. When the lungs’ natural defense and clearing systems become overwhelmed, the result is chronic airway congestion, reduced breathing capacity, and that persistent feeling of “not being able to get a full breath.”

The National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI) recognizes oxidative stress and chronic airway inflammation as key contributors to declining respiratory comfort, particularly for adults over 40 and those in urban or industrial environments.

✗ Morning chest tightness — mucus accumulation overnight restricts morning airflow
✗ Exercise breathing difficulty — reduced lung capacity limits physical activity and endurance
✗ Seasonal congestion spikes — allergens trigger airway inflammation that lingers for weeks
✗ Disrupted sleep from breathing — compromised airway comfort interrupts restful sleep cycles
✗ Fatigue from low oxygen efficiency — restricted breathing reduces energy available to cells

The Formula

Key Ingredients Inside Every
LungFresh Capsule

Each ingredient in LungFresh is selected for its documented role in respiratory support — not filler, not marketing, just purposeful nutrition for your airways.

Ingredient 01
N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC)
Mucolytic Antioxidant

NAC is a precursor to glutathione — the body’s most powerful internal antioxidant. In respiratory tissue, NAC plays two critical roles: it directly thins mucus by breaking the disulfide bonds that make it viscous, and it replenishes glutathione stores that protect bronchial lining cells from oxidative damage.

Research indexed on NIH PubMed documents NAC’s mucolytic properties and its role in airway antioxidant defense, making it one of the most studied compounds in respiratory supplement science.

Ingredient 02
Quercetin
Anti-Inflammatory Flavonoid

Quercetin is a plant-derived flavonoid with well-established anti-inflammatory and antihistamine-like properties. In airway tissue, quercetin inhibits the release of histamine and pro-inflammatory cytokines that drive seasonal and environmental reactivity in the bronchial passages.

The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements recognizes flavonoids like quercetin as bioactive compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity relevant to respiratory tissue health.

Ingredient 03
Bromelain
Proteolytic Enzyme

Derived from pineapple, bromelain is a natural proteolytic enzyme with documented mucolytic and anti-inflammatory effects in sinus and bronchial tissue. It helps break down the protein structures that make mucus thick and adhesive, complementing NAC’s mucus-thinning action through a different biochemical pathway.

Bromelain is classified by the FDA as GRAS (Generally Recognized as Safe) and is widely used in natural respiratory support formulations.

Ingredient 04
Cordyceps Mushroom Extract
Oxygen Efficiency Adaptogen

Cordyceps sinensis has been studied for its unique ability to support cellular oxygen utilization — the efficiency with which cells extract and use oxygen from the air you breathe. Athletes have used cordyceps to support breathing endurance and reduce respiratory fatigue during exertion.

Research published at NIH National Library of Medicine links cordyceps supplementation to improved VO2 max and reduced respiratory effort during physical activity.

Ingredient 05
Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
Collagen & Immune Support

Vitamin C is essential for the synthesis of collagen — the structural protein that maintains the integrity of bronchial wall tissue and alveolar membranes. It also acts as a water-soluble antioxidant in the airway fluid lining, neutralizing inhaled oxidants before they can damage tissue.

The NIH ODS Vitamin C fact sheet confirms its role in immune function and antioxidant protection of pulmonary tissue.

Ingredient 06
Ivy Leaf Extract
Traditional Airway Herb

Ivy leaf (Hedera helix) extract is one of the most widely used herbal respiratory ingredients in Europe, where it has been studied for its bronchodilatory and expectorant properties. Saponins in ivy leaf are believed to reduce surface tension in mucus, making it easier to clear from the airways naturally.

Ivy leaf extract preparations are recognized as traditional medicinal herbs with plausible respiratory mechanisms. All LungFresh botanicals are non-GMO and sourced from verified, quality-tested suppliers.
